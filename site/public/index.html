About this service

Where does the data come from?

From unclaimed dividend lists that listed companies and CMSF have published on their websites. Each entry links to the source document and page so it can be checked.

Why is the name partly hidden?

To protect shareholders' privacy, only the first letter of each word of the name is shown. The full name is in the source document.

What does “Needs check” mean?

The line in the source document could not be read with certainty, for example on a scanned page. The entry is shown so it is not missed, but it is not added to the totals.

Is my search stored?

The numbers you search for are not stored. To prevent misuse, the network address and time of each search are kept for up to two days for rate limiting.

Nothing was found. Does that mean nothing is owed?

No. Some companies publish only folio numbers, some lists could not be read automatically, and some companies have not published a list. Ask the company's share department to be sure.

Important. This is an independent prototype, not an official government service. Information is compiled from publicly published lists. It may be incomplete or out of date, and amounts may already have been paid. Always confirm with the company or CMSF before relying on any figure.
