Unclaimed Dividend Lookup

অদাবিকৃত লভ্যাংশ অনুসন্ধান

Enter a BO number to see unclaimed cash dividends, stock (bonus) shares and other items listed against it in each company.

Spaces and dashes are fine. The BO ID is on the shareholder's CDBL / brokerage statement.

Older lists (paper certificates) use folio numbers instead of BO IDs.

Please note. This information is compiled from lists that listed companies and the Capital Market Stabilization Fund (CMSF) have published. It may be incomplete or out of date, amounts may already have been claimed, and some entries are marked check because the source line could not be read with certainty. Always confirm with the company's share department or CMSF before relying on a figure.
