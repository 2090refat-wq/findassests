PROTOTYPEThis is a new service. Figures come from published lists and must be confirmed with the company.

Check for unclaimed dividends

Search the unclaimed dividend lists that listed companies and the Capital Market Stabilization Fund (CMSF) have published, using a BO ID or a folio number.

Search

The 16-digit number on your CDBL or brokerage statement. You can include spaces.
Only companies that published lists by folio number are shown.
Used for paper share certificates, for example 003155 or B02-398.

Help with this service

Where the information comes from

From unclaimed dividend lists that listed companies and CMSF have published on their websites. Each entry links to the source document and page so it can be checked.

If nothing is found

That does not mean nothing is owed. Some companies publish only folio numbers, some lists could not be read automatically, and some companies have not published a list. Ask the company's share department to be sure.

Entries marked “Check”

The line in the source document could not be read with certainty, for example on a scanned page. The entry is shown so it is not missed, but it is not added to the totals.

Why the name is partly hidden

To protect shareholders' privacy, only the first letter of each word of the name is shown. The full name is in the source document.

What happens to your search

The numbers you search for are not stored. To prevent misuse, the network address and time of each search are kept for up to two days.

This is an independent prototype, not an official government service. Information is compiled from publicly published lists. It may be incomplete or out of date, and amounts may already have been paid. Always confirm with the company or CMSF before relying on any figure.
